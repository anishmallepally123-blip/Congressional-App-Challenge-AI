How teaching works

  • The AI models in this app are downloaded unchanged from the companies that made them (Qwen from Alibaba, Gemma from Google, Llama from Meta).
  • Fully retraining a model needs a powerful graphics card and hours of computing, so here you teach it a faster way: a profile, a memory, example answers and your own assistants. The app gives these to the model with every question, so its answers change right away without changing the model's files.
  • Everything stays on this computer. Nothing you teach it is uploaded anywhere.

Ollama isn't running, so assistants can't be made right now. Everything else still works.

        Advanced: real fine-tuning
        
      
        Fine-tuning actually changes a model's weights using your examples. The app can't do this itself because it needs a strong graphics card (an NVIDIA card with 8 GB or more, or an Apple M-series Mac) and extra software. You can export your examples in the standard format that tools like Unsloth or MLX-LM read, train a small add-on called a LoRA adapter with one of them, and then load it in Ollama. See the chatbot README for the steps.
